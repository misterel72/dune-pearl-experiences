Technology at work

The digital layer behind every experience

The company offers digital services, but the systems behind them are not fully joined up. Investigate where information flows smoothly and where staff still bridge the gaps.

01

Online booking

Guests compare experiences, book, pay and change reservations online.

02

Guest app

Digital tickets, live updates, maps and multilingual visitor support.

03

Operations coordination

Teams coordinate vehicles, boats, equipment and staff across three sites. Separate departmental systems mean information is not always shared automatically.

04

Business reporting

Managers need information about demand, income, staffing, feedback and performance. Finance still manually reconciles site totals before reporting.
