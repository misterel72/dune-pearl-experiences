L02 · 50-minute lesson

Decode a client brief

Separate the problem, evidence, constraints and required decision.

Open your workbook: p106; decoding guidance p5 and company profile p4. Use task codes if your page numbers have moved.

Client evidenceYour tasks ↓Finish & keep

Your brief

The board has sent a practice request. Your job is to establish what it actually says, what remains unknown and what needs investigating.

Words to use: fact · assumption · constraint

Start here

L02-S: Without the profile, name one site, one task and one problem. Check p4, then correct one detail if needed.

Pause for your teacher’s explanation and model before beginning the main tasks.

Read & investigate

Evidence to use

Client brief

Fictional practice request · workbook p106

Marina staff re-enter guest details after an online booking. At the camp, an internet outage can prevent staff seeing updated arrival lists. The board wants a smoother guest experience without stopping service during disruption. No approved budget, measured queue time or offline procedure has been supplied. Recommend what should be investigated before selecting a system.

Use this brief for L02. Do not import figures from later workbook scenarios into this investigation.

Work in your own workbook

Your tasks

L02-A

Decode before deciding

  1. Underline facts, circle constraints and mark missing information with “?”.
  2. Complete the five workbook prompts: organisation/purpose; people/tasks/information; two problems with exact evidence; constraint; decision requested.
  3. Complete the two-row record: fact supplied, assumption to avoid and neutral question to ask.
Need a starting point?

Highlight the brief before writing. Use the five decoding prompts on p5.

Ready to go further?

Prioritise the unanswered question that would most affect your recommendation.

Finish & keep

L02-E: “The camp needs a faster internet package.” Is this established by the brief? Explain and name the evidence still needed.

Before you finish, check you have:

  • L02-A five-prompt decoding record
  • Two fact / assumption / question records
  • L02-E independent answer

Keep working in your own workbook. Do not turn in the whole workbook unless your teacher asks. If you use a separate response document, label it with the task code and link it from your workbook.
