Your brief

An impressive-sounding claim is not the same as a justified recommendation. Improve a proposal by connecting it to the client’s actual work.

Words to use: explain · consequence · limitation

Start here

L03-S: Explain the difference between a supplied fact and an assumption. Give one example from L02.

Pause for your teacher’s explanation and model before beginning the main tasks.

Read & investigate

Evidence to use

Proposal to critique

“Cloud computing will solve all the company’s problems.”

This is an untested claim for discussion, not a board decision or a confirmed finding.

  • Check the reported challenges
  • Revisit the L02 practice brief
  • Use the command words and worked model inside workbook L03.

Work in your own workbook

Your tasks

L03-A

Improve the claim

  1. Use one profile fact to improve the claim about cloud computing.
  2. Explain a possible benefit and state a limitation.
  3. Use the model in L03 to plan, then write your own paragraph in the L03-A response box.

Write in L03 → L03-A. Use the matching response box in your personal workbook.

L03-R

Respond to feedback

  1. Swap with a partner. Point to the case evidence and the “because” chain.
  2. Ask one question about a limitation.
  3. Revise one sentence and explain the change. Keep your first version visible.

Write in L03 → L03-R. Use the matching response box in your personal workbook.

Need a starting point?

Claim → case evidence → consequence → limitation. Use this to plan, not as a paragraph to copy.

Ready to go further?

Explain which extra piece of evidence would make your revised claim more convincing.

Finish & keep

L03-E: What does an explanation add beyond naming a piece of technology? Give a one-sentence example. Then record one specific learning target in the final box in L03.

Before you finish, check you have:

  • L03-A first paragraph
  • L03-R visible revision and explanation
  • L03-E answer and progress targets
