Your brief

You are a junior IT consultant. Before you recommend equipment, find out what Dune & Pearl does, who works there and which problems need investigating.

Words to use: purpose · stakeholder · evidence

Start here

L01-S: In the L01 section of your workbook, attempt D1, D2 and D7 without notes. Keep your first answers.

Pause for your teacher’s explanation and model before beginning the main tasks.

Read & investigate

Evidence to use

The company profile is inside workbook L01. Use these website sections to investigate it:

  • What the company does
  • Three sites and their work
  • Reported business challenges

These are fictional case facts. A possible effect you infer is not automatically a confirmed incident.

Work in your own workbook

Your tasks

L01-A

Build a client snapshot

  1. Record the company’s purpose and the work at each of its three sites.
  2. Select three facts that could affect its IT.
  3. Link one reported problem to a person it affects.
  4. Write one useful question to ask before choosing technology.

Write in L01 → L01-A. Use the matching response box in your personal workbook.

Discuss

Who should benefit first from an IT upgrade: guests, staff or the business? Give a reason, then record a different view you considered.

Need a starting point?

“The profile says… so I need to ask…”

Ready to go further?

Explain whose priority might conflict with the person you identified.

Finish & keep

L01-E: Why should a consultant investigate the organisation before choosing devices? Use one Dune & Pearl fact.

Before you finish, check you have:

  • L01-A client snapshot
  • A different view from the discussion
  • L01-E independent answer
