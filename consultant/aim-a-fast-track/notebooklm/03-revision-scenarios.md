# Aim A Independent Practice | Three new fictional clients
## Not a Pearson set assignment
These cases are invented for revision and discussion. None reproduces the protected live brief.

## Practice client 1 | Coastline Veterinary Centre
A small veterinary practice has two treatment rooms, a reception desk and one mobile vet. Six staff need to manage appointments, medicine stock, clinical notes and messages. Reception sometimes loses access to the booking calendar. The mobile vet needs limited authorised access while travelling. The owner has not supplied a budget or measured outage log.
**Try:** (1) Identify three different stakeholders and their requirements. (2) Select hardware and software for two roles. (3) Sketch local and remote connections. (4) Compare cloud and hybrid systems. (5) Explain two failure risks. (6) Give a supported recommendation and identify missing evidence.
**Think further:** How do privacy, offline access and staff training influence the recommendation?

## Practice client 2 | Sandstone Sports Academy
A sports academy operates an indoor training hall and an outdoor pitch. Staff register players, manage coaches' schedules, record attendance and send safety notices to families. Coaches need mobile devices outside. The office uses separate spreadsheets, and there is no agreed process when the internet fails during a tournament. No measured wireless coverage data has been supplied.
**Try:** (1) Record facts versus assumptions. (2) Plan devices, peripherals and accessible communication. (3) Describe a labelled network and suitable mobile connection. (4) Explain latency, Wi-Fi coverage and resilience. (5) Analyse positive and negative impacts for families, coaches and managers. (6) Evaluate two feasible solutions.
**Think further:** Why might a faster broadband package not solve the actual problem?

## Practice client 3 | Harbour Arts Studio
An arts studio sells workshops online, teaches in two classrooms and has a small on-site shop. Eight workers share teaching media, sell materials and manage classes. Large video files take time to upload; staff also need reliable payment terminals. Some students use assistive technologies. The owner wants new technology but has not given a cost ceiling.
**Try:** (1) Distinguish network bandwidth, throughput and latency. (2) Suggest appropriate devices and storage. (3) Compare lossless and lossy compression for different files. (4) Recommend software, permissions and backup procedures. (5) Assess a cloud-versus-on-site option. (6) Choose one emerging technology with a real benefit, or explain why none should be adopted yet.
**Think further:** Could a cheaper, simpler workflow solve more than a fashionable technology?
