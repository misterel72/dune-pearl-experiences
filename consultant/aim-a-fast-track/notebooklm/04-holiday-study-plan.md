# Half-term | Four short independent sessions
**Target:** Approximately four 35-minute sessions. This is preparation, not the live Pearson assessment.
**Set up:** Open your own copy of the approved Aim A NotebookLM notebook. Add the three teaching sources provided by school. Use the interactive Fast Track hub for tasks and recall.

### Session 1 | People, hardware and software
10 min: Read A1 to A4 of the knowledge bank. 10 min: Generate or complete flashcards. 10 min: Answer a short hardware/software scenario without NotebookLM. 5 min: Record two points to revisit.

### Session 2 | Connecting a business
10 min: Review A5 to A7 using the mind map. 10 min: Explain the difference between Wi-Fi, the internet, LAN and WAN aloud. 10 min: Work on Sandstone Sports Academy's network challenge. 5 min: Record what is still confusing.

### Session 3 | Cloud, emerging technology and judgement
10 min: Review A8 and A9. 10 min: Listen to an audio overview or read the source instead. 10 min: Compare cloud, local and hybrid solutions for Coastline Veterinary Centre. 5 min: Explain your choice with a limitation.

### Session 4 | Independent consultant challenge
5 min: Choose Harbour Arts Studio. 25 min: Close the notebook and independently produce a short solution covering software, hardware, network, stakeholders and a judgement. 5 min: Reopen the writing guide and self-assess. Make a personal revision plan, not a generated final answer.

### Confidence traffic lights
**Green:** I can explain and apply this without prompts. **Amber:** I recognise it but cannot yet use it in a new case. **Red:** I cannot explain it in my own words.
Make a green/amber/red decision for A1 to A9. Begin the return lesson with your weakest two areas.

**Important:** The official Pearson live assignment must not be placed in NotebookLM, AI chat, revision documents or holiday tasks. Follow the centre's formal assessment instructions after half-term.
