Your new client | Alder Community Library

Alder Community Library operates a main library, a small satellite reading room and a mobile outreach van. Nine employees help people borrow books, book events, use public computers and access information. Some visitors use screen readers or other assistive tools.

Library staff currently maintain separate spreadsheets of event bookings. Public visitors need access to guest Wi-Fi, while employees must protect internal records. The outreach van needs up-to-date reservation information while away from the main building. During a recent disruption, employees could not access a shared service. No documented network measurements or approved budget have been supplied.

The manager wants a coherent IT infrastructure solution that improves services without creating unacceptable risks or excluding users.

Important: The brief intentionally omits some information. Do not invent confirmed prices, throughput, outage frequency or network coverage. State what must be checked.

Produce your report

Your sections should address the entire solution. Explain features when relevant, develop impacts and reach a balanced, supported conclusion. You may use tables and a labelled network diagram in your normal workbook. The spaces here expand as you type.

State the library's main tasks and requirements. Identify three stakeholder groups and any unknowns requiring investigation.

Choose appropriate equipment and software for employees, visitors and the van. Include accessibility, integration and a possible limitation.

Explain how the sites and van could communicate, why your connection choices suit the users, and how an outage could be managed.

Analyse positive and negative impacts on staff, visitors and management. Compare at least two approaches, and give a final recommendation with tests.

Describe your network diagram using arrows and labels, or record where you saved your own drawing. Do not paste a generated report.
