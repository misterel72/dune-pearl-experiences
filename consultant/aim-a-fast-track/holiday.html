Useful NotebookLM prompts

Diagnose my weakest topic: “Ask me five questions on A5 and A6, one at a time. Wait for my answer; explain mistakes using only the uploaded sources.”
Make me think: “Give me a task from a fictional client. Ask me to choose the technology, then challenge my evidence and limitations. Do not write a final answer for me.”
Improve my evaluation: “Check whether my paragraph explains consequences and weighs alternatives. Point out missing reasoning. Do not replace my work with your own paragraph.”

Do not upload the live Pearson assignment to NotebookLM or other AI services. NotebookLM can make mistakes, so verify technical points against the source documents.
