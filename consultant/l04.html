Consultant Hub / U1-01 Start with the organisation

L04 · 50-minute lesson

Understand the work

Turn business needs into testable IT requirements.

Open your workbook: p8; Topic 01. Use task codes if your page numbers have moved.

Client evidenceYour tasks ↓Finish & keep

Your brief

The board needs IT that supports the work. Investigate business functions and the information people need before making a shopping list.

Words to use: infrastructure · requirement · stakeholder

Start here

Use START on p8. What is the difference between hardware and software? Who could be affected when a booking system fails?

Pause for your teacher’s explanation and model before beginning the main tasks.

Read & investigate

Evidence to use

Read EXPLAIN and the business-function table on workbook p8. Use the company’s locations and departments to put those functions in context.

The table includes service delivery, resources, marketing and sales, staff management, support and finance. Choose functions because of the work they do, not because of a device you want to recommend.

Work in your own workbook

Your tasks

L04

Map work to information needs

  1. Choose two different business functions from the p8 table.
  2. Annotate the task, information and person for each.
  3. Explain one way their priorities could conflict. Keep these as L04 notes.
Need a starting point?

“This team needs… to… because…” Start with the person and task.

Ready to go further?

How would you check whether your requirement had actually been met?

Finish & keep

T01-KC: Rewrite “buy fast computers” as a requirement with a user, task and success measure. Label any proposed target that has not been agreed with the client.

Before you finish, check you have:

  • L04 annotations for two functions
  • An explained conflict between priorities
  • T01-KC requirement

Keep working in your own workbook. Do not turn in the whole workbook unless your teacher asks. If you use a separate response document, label it with the task code and link it from your workbook.
