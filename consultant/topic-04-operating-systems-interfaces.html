L16 · OS services

Five jobs. One operating system.

Memory management

Allocates and manages RAM.

Multitasking

Schedules work between processes.

Device drivers

Allow the OS to communicate with hardware.

Networking services

Support communication.

Security services

Manage accounts, permissions and protections.

Consultant warning

A slow booking screen is not proof that the OS is the problem.

Challenge: choose one service and explain how it could matter during a busy camp check-in.

GUI

Visual controls and familiar workflows.

Risk: clutter and resource use

CLI

Precise administration, scripting and automation.

Risk: knowledge required; mistakes can spread

Menu-based

Restricted, repeated choices such as kiosks.

Risk: limited flexibility

Adapted

Screen reader, magnification, voice or switch access.

Must be tested with the user

L18 · Respond

The upgrade installs. The printer does not.

Compatibility risk

IT wants to update the booking laptops.

The camp’s older receipt printer may not have a compatible driver.

The evidence supports a controlled trial. Driver risk is a reason to test before wider deployment, not proof that the whole upgrade must be abandoned.
!
Diagnose and test.

An installation that completes successfully does not prove that the whole working system still works.
