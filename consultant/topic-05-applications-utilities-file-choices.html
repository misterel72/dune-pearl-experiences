L20 · Utilities

Utilities support the system around the work.

Protect

Anti-malware, encryption and firewalls.

Preserve

Backup and recovery tools.

Manage

File management, compression and network utilities.

Maintain

Disk analysis, partition tools and defragmentation utilities.

SSD check: traditional defragmentation is designed for HDDs. Do not recommend routine defragmentation for SSDs; use the operating system’s appropriate optimisation tools.

L20 · Licences & formats

Access, ownership and compatibility matter.

Open and proprietary software

Open-source: source code is available under a licence that permits specified use, study or modification. There may still be costs for support, setup, migration or hosting.
Proprietary: use is governed by the owner's licence. Fees, restrictions, updates, support and export rights vary by supplier.

Formats carry different properties

Images: JPEG, PNG, GIF, RAW, SVG

Audio/video: MP3, WAV, MPEG family; AVI is a container whose codecs affect compatibility.

Documents/data: DOC/DOCX, RTF, XLS/XLSX, XML, PDF.

Check fidelity, transparency, editing, file size, accessibility and recipient support.

1 · Calculate

L22 · Respond

Do not buy on the headline price.

Fit & functions

Which tasks must it support? Who will use it? What happens if a core function is unavailable?

Data & transition

Can records be migrated accurately? What training, testing, downtime and ongoing support are required?

Licence & exit

What do fees include? Who owns the data? What does export cost? Can the client leave with usable files?

Highest-risk unknown: the supplier has not specified its export fee. What exactly would you ask, and what evidence or contract term would make the answer usable?
?
Make questions testable.

Ask for quantities, service commitments, supported formats, migration checks and written exit terms where appropriate.
