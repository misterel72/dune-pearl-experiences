Your brief

Test whether your consulting approach works beyond Dune & Pearl, then use feedback to strengthen your earlier response.

Words to use: transfer · alternative · justification

Start here

If the organisation changes, which consulting habits should stay the same? Recall three and give an example without naming a device first.

Pause for your teacher’s explanation and model before beginning the main tasks.

Read & investigate

Evidence to use

A different organisation

Fictional transfer scenario · workbook L07

A charity distributes donated food and has volunteer staff. Which two requirements would differ from Dune & Pearl, and why? Do not simply change the company name.

Those are the supplied facts for the charity. Label any extra assumptions about its size, budget, equipment or volunteers.

For the revision task, reopen your T01-ER first draft and the Dune & Pearl case evidence.

Work in your own workbook

Your tasks

T01-TR

Transfer to a different organisation

  1. Explain two requirements that differ from Dune & Pearl.
  2. Link each difference to the charity’s work or its volunteers.
  3. Label any extra assumptions you introduce.

Write in L07 → T01-TR. Use the matching response box in your personal workbook.

T01-REVIEW

Improve the reasoning, not just spelling

  1. Check your earlier response for specific evidence, explained consequences and the stakeholder conflict.
  2. Use one precise peer question, then revise your weakest paragraph independently.
  3. Keep the original and revised wording visible. Explain one change that makes your reasoning stronger.

Write in L07 → T01-REVIEW. Use the matching response box in your personal workbook.

Discuss

Should a manager’s wish for more monitoring outweigh staff privacy? State a purpose, consider an alternative and justify your view. You do not need to share personal experiences.

Need a starting point?

Explain why the changed organisation changes the requirement. For revision, improve the reason, evidence or judgement, not just the length.

Ready to go further?

What evidence would you request from the charity before choosing a system?

Finish & keep

T01-REVIEW: Which change made your recommendation more defensible, and why? Point to the original and revised wording, then name your next step.

Before you finish, check you have:

  • T01-TR transfer response
  • T01-ER original and revised paragraph
  • T01-REVIEW explanation and next step
