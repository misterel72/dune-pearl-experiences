Consultant Hub / U1-01 Start with the organisation

L05 · 50-minute lesson

Apply the client evidence

Convert reported problems into requirement records.

Open your workbook: p9; T01-A1 and T01-A2. Use task codes if your page numbers have moved.

Client evidenceYour tasks ↓Finish & keep

Your brief

Use the client’s observations to identify requirements. Keep the supplied evidence separate from success targets you propose.

Words to use: user · task · data · success measure

Start here

“Use tablets” or “check arrivals during an outage”: which is a requirement? Explain your choice and add one question worth checking.

Pause for your teacher’s explanation and model before beginning the main tasks.

Read & investigate

Evidence to use

Organisation brief

Fictional case evidence · workbook p9

Dune & Pearl operates across three sites. Departments have bought separate systems. A guest can reserve a marina activity online, then telephone to add dinner at the camp. Staff re-enter the details. The board wants one coherent guest experience, but each site must still operate during disruption. Your first job is to understand the work before proposing a solution.

What the client reports

ObservationWhat the client reports
Booking teamStaff enter the same guest details in two systems.
Camp supervisorA connection outage can stop staff seeing updated arrival lists.
Finance teamSite totals must be manually reconciled before reporting.

Work in your own workbook

Your tasks

T01-A1

Read the case like a consultant

  1. Underline the organisation, circle three problems and identify two constraints.
  2. Separate supplied facts from assumptions you need to check.

T01-A2

Write three requirement records

  1. Create one record for each of the three observations.
  2. Include the user, task, data and success measure.
  3. Check that each record states a need, not just a product name. Label success targets that still require client agreement.
Need a starting point?

Use the five decoding prompts on p5. Organise each requirement as user → task → data → success measure.

Ready to go further?

Which requirement might conflict with another stakeholder’s need?

Finish & keep

Which part of one requirement record still needs client agreement? Explain why the missing agreement matters and add a note beside it.

Before you finish, check you have:

  • T01-A1 annotated brief
  • T01-A2 three requirement records
  • A note identifying what needs client agreement

Keep working in your own workbook. Do not turn in the whole workbook unless your teacher asks. If you use a separate response document, label it with the task code and link it from your workbook.
