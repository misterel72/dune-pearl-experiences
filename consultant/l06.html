Your brief

Investigate the camp’s needs through a short role-play interview, then explain the IT requirements of two business functions.

Words to use: neutral question · simulated evidence · analyse

Start here

“Wouldn’t a new tablet solve the camp’s problems?” Explain the problem with this question, then rewrite it to gather useful evidence.

Pause for your teacher’s explanation and model before beginning the main tasks.

Read & investigate

Evidence to use

Use the three client observations, the organisation brief and your own L05 requirement records.

Role-play is simulated evidence.

Your partner is acting as the camp supervisor. Use the supplied facts and say “unknown” where needed. Label any additional role-play answers as simulated. Do not turn invented timings, costs or outage frequencies into company facts.

Work in your own workbook

Your tasks

T01-A3

Interview the camp supervisor

  1. Prepare three neutral questions that could change your recommendation.
  2. Interview a partner acting as the camp supervisor.
  3. Record the answers and clearly distinguish supplied facts, unknowns and simulated answers.

Write in L06 → T01-A3. Use the matching response box in your personal workbook.

T01-ER

Write your first applied response

  1. Explain how two business functions create different IT requirements.
  2. Analyse one conflict between stakeholders.
  3. Recommend how it should be handled, using specific case evidence.

Write in L06 → T01-ER. Use the matching response box in your personal workbook.

Need a starting point?

Need → evidence → consequence → limitation → judgement. Use the workbook’s model to understand the reasoning, not as your own response.

Ready to go further?

Compare an alternative and explain why your preferred compromise is stronger.

Finish & keep

Underline the sentence that best explains a consequence for a stakeholder. Explain its “because” chain and mark one part to improve next lesson.

Before you finish, check you have:

  • T01-A3 questions and labelled interview record
  • T01-ER first draft
  • One identified improvement for L07
